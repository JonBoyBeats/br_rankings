# NBA per-tier ranking rules (step 6)

Written by scripts/nba_tier_rules.py. Each candidate against the Tiers button's current order inside the tier: points per pair on the decision pairs they order differently (positive: the candidate beat the button), with the 80% range from resampling slates, pairs, and the evidence level; then the candidate's own points per pair over every decision pair in the tier (the button's own is in the header). A scratch is scored through his swap. Every candidate was listed before testing, so a LEAN is adopted under the policy.
