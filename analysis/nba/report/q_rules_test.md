# Q-tag rules on points (all pairs, by nights)

Questionable at the lock: morning 76 players on 21 nights (T1-T4: 30, of whom 17 sat; T5: 46, 23 sat); afternoon 59 on 19 nights (T1-T4: 19, 10 sat; T5: 40, 17 sat). Players who sat score their swap's points. Every pair involving a Q player that the two placements order differently; a night goes to the one with more pairs right.

Rules: a = keeps his tier; b = drops two tiers; c = no higher than the top of T4; d = bottom of the list.

## In plain English

"Clearly" = one placement ahead in 90%+ of night-resamples; "leaning" = 80-90%.

- Bottom of the list (d) loses clearly to every other placement in both windows (19-2 to 20-1 nights): Q players who play outscore the tail they'd be put under.
- Keeping his tier (a) is the best of the four on points. Afternoon: ahead of "no higher than T4" clearly (7-2 nights, 98%), ahead of "drop two tiers" leaning (85%, +2.2 per pair, though "drop two" won more nights, 7-5-1). Morning: ahead of both, not clearly (76-79%).
- Drop two tiers (b) against no higher than T4 (c): no difference in either window.
- Beneficiaries: no clear lift when the Q teammate sat. Morning +1.2 points against projection over when he played (69% of resamples), afternoon -0.6 (41%). Afternoon, teammates of a Q player beat ETR by about 1.4-2.0 more than everyone else whether he sat or played.
- Samples are thin before T5: 30 morning and 19 afternoon Q players in T1-T4.
