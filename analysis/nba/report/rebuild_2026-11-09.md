## What this changes

Nothing adopted changes. The AM "no push" lean is stronger (back one tier now leans against, with the RULE numbers).
The policy label in tier_rules.md now reads "eligible (listed before testing)" instead of "ADOPTED": a lean there makes
a candidate eligible; leans.json says what is adopted, provisional or in the shadow, and everything adopted is
provisional until its money verdict. Two AM T4 alternatives now lean ahead of the current Proj (3: ADP) button: the
Boom tiebreak (+2.2, 46 pairs) and the leverage tiebreak (+2.9, 45 pairs; the one reverted on 11-09, already in the
shadow). Neither is adopted; both were picked from several, so either would be provisional and shadow-tested.
