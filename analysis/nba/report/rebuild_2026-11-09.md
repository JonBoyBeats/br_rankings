## Later the same day: note timing, the overnight window, the rebuild again

**Note timing** (scripts/nba_tier_takes.py): a note credits a take only when stamped on the slate's own Pacific day and
before that window's record (the lock, or the save it fell back to); a note without a stamp, after the record or from an
earlier day credits no one. The note files carried no time, so each got the time it was first committed to the repo (no
earlier than it was in hand): 10-20 to 10-23 were committed on 10-23's night and credit nothing; the rest were filed the
same day between 6:11am and 2:25pm, after every AM record. Takes before and after (1,739, on the first rebuild):

**The overnight window** (scripts/nba_daily.py): the AM window starts at 4:30am PT; drafts before it (the night before
included) are OVERNIGHT, labelled, kept out of the AM grades and graded on their own (the report's season section: your
board saved before your first overnight draft against ADP then). A Lock AM counts when pressed by your first draft from
4:30am (plus the half hour for hourly draft times); the AM fallback is a board saved from 4:30am. Draft times are the
nearest hourly ADP snapshot, so a draft stamped 4:23-4:27 sits on the line: 6 nights' first overnight drafts are there.
