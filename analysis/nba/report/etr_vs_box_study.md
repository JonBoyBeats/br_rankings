# ETR's final projection against the box score (study, 2026-11-13)

The afternoon question: where does ETR's final number miss in ways we can see before tip? 23 slates (10-20 to 11-12),
2,876 player-nights with ETR's final median and a played game (box files). Actual minus ETR: mean +0.13, sd 9.8.
Injury games held apart: "clean" is the player untagged at lock, no core teammate or opposing core player out, not
gone early (1,320 player-nights); the rest is 932 with a teammate out, 574 with an opposing core player out, 37 tagged.
Clean games averaged +0.19, teammate-out games +0.50, opponent-out games -0.15.

All together, fitted on earlier nights and scored on the next: ETR alone misses by 7.72 points, ETR plus the best of
these by 7.68. The box score has almost nothing systematic to add to ETR's final.

The level pattern: players ETR projects under 15 beat its median 61% of the time (+2.1 at the median); 15-46 run a
point under. Part of it is who's counted: only players who played, so a fringe player's chance of sitting, which ETR
prices, is missing here. Read it as ETR being careful with the 6th round, not as an edge.

Streaks don't carry: after 3 straight games 8+ under ETR, the next game ran +0.88 over ETR (12 of 19 beat it).
Dejounte Murray's four unders (19.4, 13.1, 16.1, 14.4 against ETR's 35-37) came in a 16-point loss, a 51-point loss
with 18 minutes and a 34-point loss: ETR holding him near 36 is what the record backs.
