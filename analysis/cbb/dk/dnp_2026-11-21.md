# Who sits? A chance-to-play for the players we project (2026-11-21)

Step B of the coordinator's follow-up to the cheap-player study (cheap_minutes_2026-11-21.md: about 312 cheap DNPs at
about 2.7 points each). Analysis only: nothing on the page or in the projections changed.
Script: `scripts/dk_dnp_study.py` (accuracy, calibration, lenses; scoring) and `scripts/dk_dnp_study.js` (the banks).

**A thin sample:** 9 slates with box scores for the chance and the accuracy; 5 nights with standings for the lineups.

## The chance to play

A small L2 logistic fit of *played* (real minutes > 0), **leave-one-slate-out**: every slate is scored by a model that
never saw it. Inputs, all before lock:
- projected minutes (log) and depth (rank on his team by projected minutes);
- season participation (his games / his team's) and games missed;
- the "missed k of n" tag (of his team's last 5 game dates, how many he missed, and the last one) and days since his
  last game. These dates are on file for 6 of the 9 slates (11-18 on); a flag marks the rest;
- Questionable, newcomer / thin sample, and teammates Out.

The current implied chance is 0 for Out, the gate's Q factor for Questionable, else 1. The variant projection is
chance x our projection (minutes if he plays x our rate), the Q discount replaced by it:
- `dnp_cheap`: under $5,000 only;
- `dnp_all`: every player.

## Verdict (A2 rule)

- **The chance itself:** clearly better than the current implied one, held out.
  - Brier on every player not Out: 0.072 vs 0.264, better on 9 of 9 slates.
  - In the draw's pool (projected 15+ minutes): 0.0145 vs 0.0249, 8 of 9.
- **Calibration:** good held out, if a little high in the 0.3–0.8 bins.
- **The Q factor of 0.353 is right on this data.** Actual / projected over the 24 Q player-nights is 0.367. They played
  38% of the time, and scored 0.75 of their projection when they did.
- **Points:** chance x projection lowers the points MAE on 9 of 9 slates (all players 5.50 -> 5.13) and the RMSE on 8
  of 9. Most of that is the deep bench, which the draw never uses.
- **Lineups:** `dnp_cheap` beat current on top 10%, cash and percentile on 3 of 5 nights. Pooled: +0.2 top 10%, +0.4
  cash, +1.5 percentile. Most of it is one night and one player.
- **Lenses:** the shape lens gets worse (|top-2 share vs recent| 0.012 -> 0.020, larger on 7 of 9), since shrinking
  the bench raises the top two's share. Unexplained salary gaps +2 (`dnp_cheap`) and +12 (`dnp_all`). Ownership shape
  is not measured (the field builder would need a rerun per variant).
- **So:** promising, but it does not pass yet. The lineup gain is 3 of 5 nights, driven by one player, and the shape
  lens is worse. `dnp_all` is worse than `dnp_cheap` on the gaps lens. Re-test as nights with standings arrive.
- **A narrower use worth testing next:** flag in-pool players with a low chance (Eddie Ricks III at 0.50) rather than
  reprice everyone, since the pool's DNPs are what cost lineups.
