Generated by `scripts/dk_zero_gp_study.py` (+ `.js` banks). Analysis only. The check before the 11-22 lock (157019).

## Why they get minutes

cbb_nightly `project()`: a returning player's minutes are `(played + MIN_FILL x mp) / (gp + MIN_FILL)`, mp last season's minutes through CARRY. At gp 0 that is mp in full. A team game he sat counts for nothing (gp counts only games he appeared in), so missing all 4-6 of his team's games leaves last season's role untouched. Obomate Abbey (UTAH, last season 15.3 min -> carried 20.2 raw, 16.2 after the team fit), Carl Cherenfant (MCNE, 16.3 -> 20.9 -> 19.1), Mark Drone (TXST, 30.4 -> 25.8 -> 25.2): all src box, prior 2025-26 at the same school, gp 0, no EvanMiya row (em_role none), dk_avg 0. Not an out boost.
