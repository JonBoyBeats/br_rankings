1. Draw lineups into the bank

Set up an idea, then Draw & bank: each lineup is the best one for a simulated night under everything here, tagged with its idea. Leverage weight (0 = off) fades the chalk inside the idea: each player loses weight × his projected ownership in points (0.1: a 30%-owned player loses 3). Use it when you like a team or stack but want the less-owned way into it, e.g. "2+ from KU" at 0.1 leans toward KU's 6%-owned guards over its 30%-owned star.

Group the bank's tags into ideas (e.g. six Kansas tags into "Kansas"): the cut works within each group. Re-sim & cut takes the bank down to Keep: each round simulates the survivors over fresh nights and adds each lineup's payout alone in every contest you're entering to its running total; a lineup's rank is its best in ANY of them. Cut: by merit (floor 5), the default: every lineup is ranked together and the best overall survive, Cut % a round down to Keep, each group keeping at least its best max(5, 1% of its lineups) so no idea vanishes. Equal share per idea (for comparison): each group keeps the same share of its own lineups (Keep / bank), its best-ranked. Either way a lineup sharing 7+ of 8 players with a better one its group kept is skipped. Build on a contest opens its builder below (your picks save as you go); Fill all contests builds a starting portfolio for every contest instead (Start from: Max ROI, Balanced or Max coverage), within Max exposure, each player's Max%, Min unique and Max core; each lineup goes in one contest only. Export DK entries fills the entries file for upload; Save build downloads the bank and portfolios to send in with the standings.

3. Build a contest's portfolio

The builder's columns
Press Build on a contest above. Tier: A / B / C, split where lineups differ by more than the sim's own noise (its odd nights against its even), so lineups in one tier are a toss-up; ROI %: its expected payout alone less the fee, over the fee; Top 10% / Top 1% / Cash: how often it gets there alone; Added ROI: what it adds to the set (with it less without: your lineups compete with each other), over the fee; +Cover: the rise in nights at least one of yours reaches the top 1% (or cashes); Shared: the most players it shares with a pick; Own: its players' projected ownership summed; Core %: the simulated field's share holding its three highest salaries; Dupes: field entries expected to hold the identical lineup. Click an idea to highlight its lineups and bring them to the top. Auto-fill adds by Added within Max exposure, Min unique and Max core. Picks save to the contest as you go.
Tap: with (only lineups with it), again: without, again: off. Counts: lineups in view / your picks.
