Players

Proj is ours in DraftKings points, with the double-double bonus at his chance of one (shown as DD when 10%+). Value is Proj per $1,000. Type over a Proj to use your own number. L locks a player into every lineup, X leaves him out. Max% caps one player below the build's max exposure. Out players are left out. ± is how far off we expect to be (an average miss, DraftKings points); fragile marks players whose expected miss runs 1.31x+ a typical player's at the same projection: minutes that swing game to game, few games, a bench role, a transfer. On 2025-26 that third missed by 49% of its projection, the rest by 35%.

Build lineups

Team and if/then rules: every lineup obeys them (Build only, Sim & pick, the builder). Team tiles add them in one click.
Build only: each lineup is the highest-projected one that fits the $50,000 cap and G, G, G, F, F, F, UTIL, UTIL and the rules above, after Randomness nudges every Proj up or down (a standard deviation of that %, drawn fresh each lineup), sharing at most 8 − Min unique players with any lineup before it, with no player past his exposure cap. Sim & pick: builds Candidates lineups that way (no exposure cap; each differs from the others by 2+ players, at 20% randomness, its own setting), then plays them against the contest's field over Sims simulated nights and keeps the Lineups that add the most expected payout, one at a time, within the exposure cap and Min unique. A simulated night draws each player's points from his Proj and his ±, in the shape 2025-26's misses took (a long upside tail); players' nights are drawn independently, because on 2025-26 teammates' and opponents' misses were uncorrelated (−0.007 and −0.004). The field is 1,000 lineups built from a field projection (half ours, half DraftKings' season average, with noise), copied to fill the contest; Own % is their ownership: a guess until the standings files calibrate it. Payouts are the contest's own table. Export writes DraftKings' upload file (player IDs under G, G, G, F, F, F, UTIL, UTIL): Lineups → Upload Lineups on DraftKings.

Exposure

Bank, cut and portfolios

As you work the slate, bank lineups (Bank these, or Draw & bank under the current rules); each is tagged with the rules it was built under, and a lineup banked twice is kept once. Load DraftKings' entries file (Lineups → Edit Entries → download) and each contest's entry count fills in. Re-sim & cut takes the bank down to Keep: each round simulates the survivors over fresh nights and adds each lineup's payout alone in every contest you're entering to its running total (so its number is the mean over every night it has been through); a lineup stays if it ranks high enough in ANY of them, and Cut % of the rest go. After each round, the survivors by rule tag. Pick portfolios picks each contest's entries from the final pool as a set (the same pick as Sim & pick, with that contest's payouts); Review opens one in the builder below to add or remove lineups; Export DK entries fills the entries file's rows with the lineups, ready to upload (Edit Entries → upload).

Portfolio builder

Hand-build the set from a large pool. Every pool lineup is scored by what it adds to your picks so far, re-scored after every add or remove: Added is the set's expected payout with it minus without it, your own lineups ranked against each other on every simulated night (one that scores on the same nights as a pick mostly pushes yours down a place, so it adds little); +Cover is the rise in the share of nights where at least one of your lineups reaches the top 1% (or cashes, with the toggle). Alone is its expected payout by itself; Shared, the most players it shares with any pick. Uses the contest, the rules above (the pool is drawn under them) and Sims. Auto-fill adds by Added value within Max exposure and Min unique. Save build downloads the pool, your hand-built set and an auto-pick from the same pool: send it in with the standings and it gets graded, slate by slate, against the auto-pick and against random sets from the pool.
